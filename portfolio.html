technical case study

Bromford Viaduct — incident detection

Led an independent technical investigation into improving incident detection capability at Bromford Viaduct on the M6 (Junctions 5–6), a strategically important but heavily constrained section of motorway flagged in National Highways' Smart Motorway Safety Evidence Stocktake and Action Plan.

  • Assessed incident detection technologies — AI video analytics, edge-processing cameras, Floating Vehicle Data, radar, and data fusion platforms — against operational effectiveness, integration requirements and long-term maintainability.
  • Built structured comparative evaluation tables in Excel to support engineering judgement where quantitative performance data was limited.
  • Concluded that no single technology could solve the problem alone, and recommended a layered approach combining infrastructure-based detection, AI-assisted monitoring and data-driven traffic intelligence.
  • Delivered a refined shortlist of operationally and technically viable technologies, balancing performance against cost, deployment feasibility and disruption.
